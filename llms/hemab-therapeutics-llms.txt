## Therapeutic areas

Glanzmann Thrombasthenia; von Willebrand Disease; Factor VII deficiency; Bernard-Soulier
Syndrome; heavy menstrual bleeding associated with inherited bleeding disorders. Named clinical
candidates appearing in Hemab's own 2026 disclosures include sutacimig (HMB-001) and HMB-002.
